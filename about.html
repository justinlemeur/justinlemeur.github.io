ABOUT

Music in service
of the story.

I'm Justin LE MEUR, a composer focused on orchestral video game music for story-driven RPG and action-adventure experiences.

My goal is simple: create music that feels inseparable from the world it belongs to. Whether it is the first view of a new landscape, a character's defining moment, or the battle that changes everything, the score should make the player feel the weight of the journey.

I work across emotional, exploratory, cinematic and epic orchestral writing, always looking for a strong melodic identity and a sense of narrative progression.

“The best soundtrack doesn't just accompany a world.
It becomes part of the memory of it.”
